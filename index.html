Multiplayer Synth

Two players, blue plays the left half, yellow plays the right half.

  • The top two rows play the notes.
  • The bottom row changes your own sound: brightness, wave and echo.
  • While you hold a note, it also changes your partner's sound.
  • Blue notes make yellow wobble. The higher the blue note, the faster the wobble.
  • Yellow notes make blue sound crunchy. The higher the yellow note, the more crunch.
  • Let go of all your notes and your partner's sound goes back to normal.
  • The boxes at the top show what your partner is doing to your sound right now.

Try answering each other's notes, or holding a note to change your partner's sound while they play.
