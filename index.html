Multiplayer Synth

Two players, blue plays the left half, yellow plays the right half.

  • The top two rows play the notes.
  • The bottom row changes your own sound: brightness, wave and echo.
  • Your notes also affect your partner's sound. Blue makes yellow wobble, yellow makes blue sound distorted.
  • The boxes at the top show what your partner is doing to your sound.

Try answering each other's notes, or holding a note to change your partner's sound while they play.
